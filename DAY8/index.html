Comparison Operators in JS 😉

Broadly they're of two types as given below**

✅ Equality Operators:

== - equal to [Simple Equality] (Automatic conversion)

=== - equal to & type check [Strict Equality] (Manual Conversion)

!= - not equal to [Simple Equality]

!== - - not equal to & different type [Strict Not-Equal]

🔢 Relational Operators

> - greater than

< - less than

<= - less than or equal to

>= - greater than or equal to
