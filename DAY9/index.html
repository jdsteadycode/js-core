Logical Operators 🧠

  • && - Logical AND Operator ✅
  • || - Logical OR Operator 🔄
  • ! - Logical NOT (Negation) Operator ❌

&& and || operators are not limited to Boolean values (true, false)!

They also work with Truthy and Falsy values! 🎯

Examples: 0, 'Hello', undefined, NaN

Check out script.js for detailed explanations and examples! 🚀✨
